📖 About Sabda Ghar

Sabda Ghar means "House of Words." It helps people save and learn Doteli.

1. 🌱 The Problem

Doteli has few learning tools. Many words are passed by speaking. Some may be forgotten if they are not saved.

  • Few Doteli dictionaries and lessons
  • Different words in different places
  • Few voice recordings
  • Young people may miss words from older generations

2. 💡 Our Inspiration

Every word carries a piece of home.

We wanted a simple place where Doteli speakers can share and save their words.

3. 📚 Our Solution

Search words, add local forms, record voices and practise with picture lessons.

6. 🛠️ How It Is Made

Website: HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

Server: Node.js.

Word list: a JSON file. Audio: Cloudinary online storage.

The app can save some files on your device for offline use. It does not use XAMPP or SQL.

7. 🔄 How It Works

Browser
  ├── Shows words and lessons
  └── Records voices
       ↓
Node.js server
  ├── Finds and saves words
  └── Sends audio to Cloudinary

8. ✨ What Makes It Special

  • Local speakers add words and voices.
  • Local word differences are welcome.
  • Checked words are marked.
  • Picture lessons make learning fun.
  • Some saved parts work offline.

9. ❤️ How It Helps

Families can share words. Young people can learn from elders. Schools can use the lessons. Anyone with a phone or computer can open the app.
