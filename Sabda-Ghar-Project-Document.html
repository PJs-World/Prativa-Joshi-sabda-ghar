The problem

1 minute

Doteli is spoken in western Nepal, but there are few easy ways to learn it or save local words and voices.

One recent source lists about 495,000 Doteli speakers. This is one estimate, not proof that the number is falling. We do not have a trusted, like-for-like count across years to show the change.

~495,000speaker estimate

Why act? When words are not written or recorded, young people may not hear or learn them.

Our solution

2 minutes

Sabda Ghar means "House of Words." It gives Doteli words and voices a place to live.

  • Search in English, Nepali or Doteli.
  • Add a local word and the place it is used.
  • Record a speaker saying the word.
  • Let trusted reviewers check entries.
  • Learn with picture cards and short lessons.

Different local words are welcome. The app does not force every place to use the same word.

Languages and tools

1 minute

It supports English, Nepali, Roman Nepali and Doteli.

It is a website made with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. A Node.js server handles word requests. Words are saved in a JSON file; audio is stored on Cloudinary. Some app files can be saved on a device for offline use.

Why it matters

2 minutes

Families can share words. Young people can learn from elders. Schools can use the lessons. Local speakers can help check what is correct.

Next, we want to add more words, invite more speakers and reviewers, and work with schools and local groups.
