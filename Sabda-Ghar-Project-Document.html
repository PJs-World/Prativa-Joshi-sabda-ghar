Executive Summary

Sabda Ghar means "House of Words." It is a digital community dictionary designed to preserve and teach Doteli, the language of Nepal's Sudurpashchim Province.

It connects English, Nepali and Doteli in one searchable learning system. Users can search words, listen to recordings, add regional forms, contribute audio and learn vocabulary through picture cards.

1. Problem

Doteli is widely spoken in everyday conversation, but it has limited written and digital learning resources. Many regional words live mainly in speech and are passed between relatives and communities.

  • Lack of accessible digital dictionaries
  • Few standard tools for Doteli learning
  • Regional differences in vocabulary and pronunciation
  • Limited audio resources
  • Risk of vocabulary loss among younger generations
  • Few platforms allowing native-speaker contributions

2. Inspiration

Every word carries family memory, place and identity.

The project places language preservation in the hands of the community. It is inspired by community encyclopedias, audio archives, visual learning applications and Nepal's linguistic diversity.

3. Solution

Dictionary

Users search English, Nepali, Roman Nepali or Doteli and view multiple regional Doteli forms.

Community contributions

Users add Doteli text, a district or area, a contributor name and an optional recording.

Verification

Reviewer mode lets trusted users verify text and recordings and remove inappropriate content.

Learning

The Learn section uses picture cards, lesson categories, XP, hearts and unlockable progression.

6. Technology Stack

Frontend

HTML5, CSS3, vanilla JavaScript, browser Speech Synthesis, MediaRecorder and Progressive Web App features.

Backend

Node.js using the built-in HTTP module, custom API routes and dotenv configuration.

Storage

JSON file storage, browser localStorage and Cloudinary for audio. The prototype does not use XAMPP or SQL.

Offline

Service Worker caching, saved word data, PWA installation and a local progress store.

8. What Makes It Unique

  • Community ownership: native speakers contribute words and voices.
  • Regional variants: one English word can contain several local forms.
  • Visible verification: draft, community, verified and audio states are distinct.
  • Voice preservation: human pronunciation is stored as part of the archive.
  • Game-like learning: XP, hearts and lessons encourage repeat use.
  • Offline resilience: cached data remains available with limited connectivity.
  • Lightweight access: ordinary browsers and phones can run the app.

9. Social Impact

Cultural preservation

Sabda Ghar creates a digital archive before local expressions become limited to a few speakers.

Intergenerational connection

Children and young people can learn through family language and community recordings.

Education

Teachers and students receive a locally relevant language-learning resource.

Digital inclusion

The installable website works on mobile devices without a separate app-store download.
