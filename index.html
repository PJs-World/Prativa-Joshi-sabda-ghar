Doteli

A language that mostly lives in conversation

Doteli is spoken across Sudurpashchim Province, but it has very few written or digital resources. Most of its words are passed on by voice, from elders to children, and each district says things a little differently.

Sabda Ghar is a place to keep those words. Every Doteli entry comes from a community member, records the area it belongs to, and is marked verified only after someone who knows the language has checked it.

How it works

  1. 1

    Search

    Type in English, Nepali, Roman letters, or Doteli. Spelling variants are matched for you.

  2. 2

    Listen

    Hear Nepali read aloud or play a community-recorded Doteli voice while it awaits review.

  3. 3

    Contribute

    Know the Doteli word? Add it with your district and name. One word can have several local forms.

  4. 4

    Verify

    Reviewers confirm entries, so learners can tell checked words from new ones.
